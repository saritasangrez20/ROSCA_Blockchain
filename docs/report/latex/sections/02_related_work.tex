\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Table~\ref{tab:related} compares the closest work with ours. We do not claim to be the first
blockchain ROSCA; our contribution is collateral scaled to payout position with automatic slashing,
a payout draw that resists rigging, an explicit threat model backed by attack tests, and measured
gas savings.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering\small
\caption{Related work and how this project differs}
\label{tab:related}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}P{3.6cm}LL@{}}
\toprule
Work & What it does & How we differ \\
\midrule
Sharma and Chandrakar (2025) \cite{sharma2025} &
Scores the trustworthiness of ROSCA \emph{operators} with fuzzy logic and stores a time stamped trust
index on a permissioned blockchain, with a key agreement protocol. &
They keep an operator and rate it; we remove the operator's control of money. Their trust index
idea appears as our \code{ReputationRegistry}, on a public chain. \\
Sharma and Chandrakar (2024), TruChit \cite{sharma2024} &
A blockchain chit fund (ROSCA) with a machine learning creditworthiness score. &
We secure the pot with collateral instead of predicting who will default; the two could be combined. \\
Batool et al. (2023), B-ROSCA \cite{batool2023} &
Smart contract collateral on Ethereum and social network analysis to form groups of strangers. &
B-ROSCA style flat collateral loses the credit benefit; ours is scaled to payout position (100\% of
what is still owed after the payout), slashed automatically, with a fair draw and attack tests.
Member matching is future work. \\
WeTrust Trusted Lending Circles (2017) \cite{wetrust2017} &
An early Ethereum ROSCA for groups of friends, with a bidding mechanism. &
WeTrust assumed members trust each other and had no cover for a member who takes the pot and
leaves; that is exactly the case our deposit rule covers. \\
Mehmood et al. (2019) \cite{mehmood2019} &
User studies of committees in Pakistan and a digital ROSCA prototype for low literacy users. &
Informs our interface (plain language, reminders before deadlines, visible payment status). We ran
no user study. \\
Hackathon prototypes (Tanda, ROSA, Starosca) &
Collateral based ROSCAs on various chains. &
Not reviewed in depth; we focus on position scaled collateral, the threat model and gas. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
